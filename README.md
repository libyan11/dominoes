# Dominoes

## ▶ [Play the game](https://libyan11.github.io/dominoes/)

https://libyan11.github.io/dominoes/

Dominoes in your browser, on phone or computer. Play online with friends, or against the computer. Part of [Game Night](https://libyan11.github.io/).

**Play with friends:** one person taps *Create a room* and shares the 4-letter code or link. Everyone else types the code and taps *Join room*. 2 to 4 players, and the host can fill empty seats with computer players. If someone drops out, the computer plays for them until they come back.

**Rules**
- Double-six set, 7 tiles each. With 2 or 3 players the rest form the pile.
- First round: the highest double opens. After that, the last round's winner opens.
- Play a tile on either end; touching halves must match.
- Can't play? **Draw** game: take from the pile until you can (pass if it's empty). **Block** game: pass.
- Play your last tile to win the round and score the pips left in everyone else's hands.
- Blocked game: fewest pips wins and scores the others' pips; a tie scores nothing.
- First to the target (50, 100 or 150) wins the match.
